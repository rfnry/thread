# Refunds

1. A customer may return an item for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. The delivery date
   is the one on the invoice; the order date does not start the clock.
2. After 30 days, deny the refund and offer a warranty repair instead.
3. The receipt and the invoice must agree on the order number and the total. If they do not,
   escalate: it may be fraud, and a support agent does not accuse a customer.
4. Refunds go back to the card that paid. Never refund to another card or as cash.
