# Damaged on arrival

1. An item reported damaged within 7 days of delivery is refunded in full, shipping included, and
   the customer does not return it at their own cost; we send a prepaid label.
2. Damage needs photo evidence of the damage itself. A receipt or an invoice proves the purchase,
   not the damage. Without a photo of the damage, ask the customer for one before deciding.
3. Reported after 7 days, damage is handled as an ordinary refund under rules/refunds.md.
